About Hebron

Background: Pastor Ron Mamo, a church trustee and overseer of ministry departments, began his ministry around 1990 by preaching and teaching the Gospel in his hometown. He has since been engaged in the work of leading many into the Kingdom of God and equipping them to serve as Kingdom workers; he also provides spiritual and social services through the churches he has established in Addis Ababa, Bishoftu, and Adama.
